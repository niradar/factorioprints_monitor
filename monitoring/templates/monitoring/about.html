About

Factorioprints Monitor

Factorioprints Monitor watches the blueprints you publish on factorioprints.com and tracks new comments — so you can see at a glance what still needs a reply, and how your favourites and comments grow over time.

What it does
  • An inbox of new comments to reply to, with mark-as-done so your queue stays clear.
  • Per-blueprint favourites and comments, charted over time.
  • Point-in-time snapshots, taken on demand or on a schedule, via a headless browser.
  • Optional email alerts when a snapshot finds new comments.
